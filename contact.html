Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Common queries regarding commercial HVAC installations, preventative maintenance, Carrier & Toshiba systems, and operations across Tanzania.

Who is the leading commercial HVAC contractor in Tanzania? expand_more
REMCO (International) Ltd is one of Tanzania's foremost commercial and industrial HVAC contractors. With over 28 years of engineering excellence, REMCO serves as the authorized dealer for Carrier and Toshiba, delivering turnkey engineering, VRF installations, chiller systems, and 24/7 preventative maintenance across Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Arusha, and throughout East Africa.
What HVAC systems and brands does REMCO supply and install in Tanzania? expand_more
REMCO supplies, engineers, and installs world-class HVAC solutions from global leaders Carrier and Toshiba. Our systems include Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF/VRV), air-cooled and water-cooled chillers, rooftop packaged units, cleanroom Air Handling Units (AHUs), close control precision cooling for data centers, and Building Management Systems (BMS).
Does REMCO provide 24/7 HVAC maintenance and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) in Dar es Salaam? expand_more
Yes, REMCO provides 24/7 emergency repair support, comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), genuine OEM spare parts replacement, and proactive predictive maintenance for commercial office complexes, hospitals, hotels, banking facilities, and industrial warehouses across Tanzania.
Can REMCO design custom cooling systems for tropical and coastal climates like Dar es Salaam? expand_more
Yes. Coastal Tanzania requires specialized engineering due to high ambient humidity and salt corrosion. REMCO designs systems with anti-corrosion coil coatings (Blygold / Blue Fin), high-ambient tropicalized compressors, and smart moisture extraction to deliver uninterrupted efficiency and longevity.
Which regions in Tanzania does REMCO serve? expand_more
Headquartered on Samora Avenue in Dar es Salaam, REMCO operates across mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar, executing premier commercial projects in Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Arusha, Mwanza, Morogoro, and Tanga.
