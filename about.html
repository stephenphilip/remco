Who We Are

Decades of Engineering
Reliability

REMCO (INTERNATIONAL) LTD., specializes in Heating, Ventilation and Air-Conditioning (HVAC) solutions with a strong network of associates across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Founded in 1997, we combine global expertise with local insight to provide high-quality cooling products and solutions.

Through strategic partnerships with brands such as Carrier & Toshiba, we are able to offer advanced HVAC technologies at competitive rates while maintaining the highest standards of performance and reliability.

With over 28 years of industry experience, we have built a strong reputation for professionalism, quality service, and dependable solutions. Our diverse clientele includes government institutions, hospitals, banks, and major hotel & resort chains across Tanzania.

We remain committed to delivering innovative, energy-efficient HVAC solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients while maintaining the highest standards of service and technical excellence.
