HVAC System Supply & Installation

We supply a wide range of HVAC systems from leading global brands such as Carrier & Toshiba. Our experienced technical team ensures professional installation, delivering properly configured systems that achieve optimal performance, energy efficiency and long-term reliability.

Our expertise covers a wide range of HVAC systems, including Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) systems, chiller systems, packaged units, close control units, Air Handling Units (AHU), and Building Management Systems (BMS).

Maintenance & Servicing

Regular maintenance is essential for efficient system operation. We offer scheduled maintenance and servicing to keep HVAC systems running smoothly and to extend their lifespan.

Our preventative programs include thorough inspections, system cleaning, and calibration, ensuring maximum efficiency and significantly reducing the risk of unexpected breakdowns.

Repair & Troubleshooting

Our technicians provide fast and reliable repair services, identifying and resolving HVAC system related issues, minimizing downtime and ensuring quick restoration of comfort.

Equipped with advanced diagnostic tools, our rapid-response team is capable of tackling both minor fixes and major overhauls across all commercial and industrial HVAC brands.

System Design & Consultation

We assist client in selecting and designing HVAC solutions, tailored to their specific requirements, ensuring efficient climate control for building & projects of any scale.

Our engineering consultants provide end-to-end support, from initial load calculations and efficiency modeling to drafting blueprints that integrate seamlessly with your architecture.

Spare Parts Supply

We supply genuine HVAC spare parts and components to support the maintenance and repair of various cooling systems.

By maintaining a robust inventory and a reliable global supply chain, we ensure rapid access to critical OEM parts, minimizing operational delays for your facilities.

Custom Cold Room Solutions

Our team provides tailor made cold room solutions, specific to our client’s storage requirement. Our team designs and installs efficient, reliable cold storage systems suitable for applications such as food preservation, pharmaceuticals, hospitality and industrial uses.

From system planning and equipment selection to installation, we ensure optimal temperature control, energy efficiency, and long-term performance.
